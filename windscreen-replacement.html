The Job

What To Expect

A windscreen replacement is a straightforward job when it is done properly. Here is how ours run.

1
Ring Us With The Registration

The reg tells us the exact glass your vehicle takes, including whether it has sensors, heating or a rain sensor behind the mirror.

2
We Book You In

Most jobs go in same day or next day. We come to you, so the vehicle does not need moving anywhere.

3
Old Glass Out, Frame Prepared

The damaged screen is cut out and the bonding surface is cleaned back and primed. Skipping this is why cheap screens leak.

4
New Screen Bonded In

The new glass goes in, is lined up and left to cure. We tell you how long before the vehicle can be driven and what to watch for in the first day.
