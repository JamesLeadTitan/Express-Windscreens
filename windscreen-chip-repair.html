Repair Or Replace

Can Your Chip Be Repaired?

It comes down to three things. How big the damage is, where it sits on the screen, and whether that part of the glass is doing a job for a camera or a display. The repair trade works to a British Standard that spells all three out.

The Standard: BS AU 242b:2022

Windscreen repairs in this country are carried out to British Standard BS AU 242b:2022. It splits the swept area of the screen into two zones and sets a size limit for each.

Zone A is the driver's view. A 400mm wide band directly in front of the driver, centred on the steering wheel and inside the top arc of the wiper sweep. Damage there can be repaired if the whole of it fits inside a 20mm circle.

Zone B is the rest of the swept area. Damage there can be repaired if it fits inside a 40mm circle.

Camera and head-up display zones are out altogether. No repair of any kind is done in the part of the screen supporting driver assistance cameras and sensors, or a head-up display, whatever the size of the chip. Those zones vary from one make to the next and are often larger than the mirror housing suggests.

The MOT is a separate test with its own limits, and in the 290mm strip in front of the steering wheel it fails damage over 10mm. That is tighter than the 20mm the repair standard allows. A finished repair is judged on whether it still obstructs your view, and most repairs leave something you can see if you look for it, so in that strip it comes down to the tester on the day. Nobody honest will promise you a pass on it.

If It Cannot Be Repaired

When The Screen Has To Come Out

Some damage is past filling. If any of this sounds like your screen, it is a replacement rather than a repair, and that part is our work.

1
A Crack Running To The Edge

Once damage reaches the bonded edge of the glass the screen has lost strength there. Filling it does not put that back.

2
Too Big, Or Sat In Your Eyeline

Over 20mm in the 400mm strip in front of the steering wheel, or over 40mm anywhere else in the swept area, and you are into new glass under the repair standard.

3
Several Breaks Close Together

Two chips within a few centimetres can be treated as one damaged area, which pushes it over the limit even though each looks small.

4
It Has Already Spread

Cold nights, hot demisters and a rough road turn a chip into a crack. Once it has run, the repair window has gone.

5
It Sits In A Camera Or Display Zone

The area of glass working for the driver assistance cameras and sensors, or for a head-up display, is excluded from repair completely. Size does not come into it. That one is a new screen, and the cameras are recalibrated after the fit.
