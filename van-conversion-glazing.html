The Job

What To Expect

Twenty years of doing this means the job runs to a routine. Here is how a typical van window conversion goes.

1
You Ring Us With The Van Details

Make, model, year and which windows you want. We tell you what glass is available for that van and what it will cost.

2
We Come To You

Most conversions are done at your home or your yard. Our vans carry the glass, the bonding kit and everything else needed for the job.

3
The Aperture Is Cut

The panel is marked out and cut properly, then the edges are treated so the metal is sealed and protected before anything goes near it.

4
Glass Bonded And Finished

The window is bonded in, lined up and left to cure. You get told how long to leave it before the van goes back into proper use.
